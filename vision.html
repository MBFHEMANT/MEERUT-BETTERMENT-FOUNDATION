Our Vision

Why MBF Exists

Meerut is a city with enormous potential, yet many public spaces suffer from littering, lack of civic responsibility and environmental neglect. Meerut Betterment Foundation was created to inspire citizens to take responsibility and work together to improve our city.

Our Mission

To build a cleaner, greener, better and civilized Meerut through volunteer-driven initiatives, environmental action, awareness campaigns and community participation.

Our Core Values

  • Integrity
  • Transparency
  • Respect for Nature
  • Community Service
  • Responsibility
  • Teamwork

Our Dream

We dream of a Meerut where every citizen actively contributes to cleanliness, greenery, civic responsibility and sustainable development. This is not just an organization. It is a movement.
