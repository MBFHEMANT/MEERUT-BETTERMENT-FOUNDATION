Why Meerut Betterment Foundation?

A cleaner city doesn't happen by chance. It happens when ordinary citizens decide to take responsibility.

Our mission is simple — build a movement where every resident can contribute, whether by volunteering, planting trees, spreading awareness or simply keeping their surroundings clean.

Why Not Meerut?

Indore became India's cleanest city because its citizens participated.

We believe Meerut can achieve the same— not by waiting for someone else, but by building a community that takes responsibility.

Meerut Betterment Foundation is building an ecosystem where every citizen can volunteer, organize, donate, inspire and create lasting change.
